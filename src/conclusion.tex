\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \sysname, a masked-convolution runtime that replaces the dense
tiled pipeline's contiguous coordinate stream with an active-output worklist,
computing almost exclusively the positions an upstream mask requires while
retaining the dense tensor layout and the regular per-position reduction. The
construction and consumption stages are co-designed to secure both
useful-compute ratio and throughput; our ablation confirms that disabling any
single mechanism degrades one axis without improving the other. The resulting
higher effective throughput lets \sysname convert upstream mask sparsity into
proportional latency and energy reduction for the latency-critical vision
tasks where masked convolution applies.
