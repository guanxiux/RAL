\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \sysname, which establishes worklist-driven tiled execution
for masked convolution: an active-output worklist drives the dense tiled
pipeline over an input that stays on the dense grid. Because only output rows
are indexed, a worklist tile executes the instructions and SM cycles of a
dense tile, and the remaining gap to ideal scaling is a per-layer fixed cost.
\sysname co-designs worklist construction and consumption around this compute
stage; our ablation confirms that disabling any single mechanism degrades one
axis without improving the other. Together these mechanisms turn upstream mask sparsity into lower latency and energy for the
latency-critical vision tasks where masked convolution applies.
